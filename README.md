# COLD CASE OPS-01 — Anti-gravity claims cannot even be audited: the data was never publishable-grade

Family: fringe-physics  |  Disposition: **POSTABLE_AS_COLD_CASE**  |  claim_authority=NONE
Members: q11-a11-martins-fulltext-recovery-v3, q9-propellantless-thrust-compiled-table-confirmation-v2, antigrav-selected-routes-gate-chain-v1, basanos-discovery-drift-audit-20260821

## The case
Bo pointed the lab at anti-gravity and propellantless-thrust claims with a simple demand: audit them like anything else. The campaign generated 14 routes and found the field's evidentiary floor missing.

## How it died
The compiled thrust table's held-out confirmation could completely read one of 21 candidate papers - the rest blocked, unreadable, or unlocated; the experimental routes lacked the individual unprocessed channels and row-level trial ledgers their frozen questions required; the key literature-recovery route died on an anonymous rate-limited API it had no authority to credential; the warp-referee route was already covered by equivalent public work. One drift audit also caught the campaign store itself under-enforcing the mission (repaired upstream).

## What still stands
A route-by-route map of what evidence each claim class WOULD need: raw force-channel data, trial ledgers, vacuum controls with ion-wind exclusion. Eleven parked cracks remain queued. Ceiling: audit-readiness.

## What would revive it
Primary data from anyone who has run a thruster experiment: unprocessed force-balance channels, per-trial ledgers, air-versus-vacuum runs - or lawful full text of the blocked Martins corpus (an OpenAlex API key would do it).

## Card fields (G18 contract)
- plain_meaning: We tried to give anti-gravity claims a fair mechanical audit and could not, because the published record does not carry the raw measurements auditing requires. Anyone holding bench data can change that.
- what_it_is_not: Not a debunking, because claims were never tested - only their auditability was; and not an endorsement, because a field whose claims cannot be audited from its publications has a serious problem regardless.
- next_decisive_test: A single-source asymmetric-capacitor force test, air versus vacuum, with ion wind excluded - frozen and waiting for data.
- what_we_need: Bench experimenters' raw channels and trial ledgers; institutional access to the paywalled Martins corpus; an OpenAlex key.
